# fasadanakljuc.si: build brief

For the development team. Generated by `handoff-plan` from the analysis in tl-product-ideas and copied to the product repository as `docs/brief.md` by `handoff-apply`. The product repository is public: no client contacts, budget, prices, offers or personal names.

## What to build

A new site in Slovenian about facade work: it explains, step by step, how a facade is renovated, or built turnkey on a new house, and leads the visitor to a quote request. It is built on `tl-template-website` in the repository `astragroup-website-fasadanakljuc`. There is no site on the domain today.

The site is run by a construction company in the Coastal-Karst region but does not show the company's brand: no logo, link or mark of it. Only the imprint names the company, as the law requires.

Its readers:

- homeowners renovating a facade, who want to understand the steps and the Eko sklad subsidy, and ask for a quote;
- people building a new house, who want to see what a turnkey facade includes, and ask for a quote.

Everything leads to one goal: a quote request, sent by a short multi-step form, or by e-mail or phone. The form is sent by Trislab's shared form Worker to the company's mailbox; nothing is stored. There is no sign-in, no price list and no stored data.

## Design direction

The template is only the frame (build, checks, languages, legal pages, hosting). The look is the site's own, in its `src/` (components, layouts, pages, styles). A site that only went through init still looks like the template's sample. It must also look clearly different from the company's main site (navy and amber) and from its roof site (slate and copper).

- **Feel:** calm, light and practical, taken from a facade's materials: render, mineral, stone. A guide, not an advert: the visitor understands the job and then asks. The process is drawn as a numbered line, not as icons in a grid.
- **Colours** (every text and button pair passes 4.5:1):
  - warm render white `#FAF7F1` as the page background;
  - deep mineral green `#2E5A4F` as the main colour: headings, links and dark sections (7.3:1 on the background, 7.8:1 on white);
  - ochre `#8A5E12` as the accent for the calls to action (white text on it 5.7:1; as text on the background 5.3:1);
  - stone grey `#3C403D` for text (9.9:1) and `#6B6F6A` for secondary text (4.8:1);
  - a dark green band `#1F3D35` for the footer and the final call, with white text (11.8:1) and a light ochre `#E8C98A` for highlights (7.4:1).
- **Type:** one sans-serif family for text and a slightly more characterful one for headings, both open-licence and self-hosted; large step numerals.
- **Photos:** stock photos whose licence allows commercial use without attribution: facades in progress (scaffolding, insulation boards, render) and finished houses in a Mediterranean or Karst setting. No photo pretends to be a job the company did, and none shows a recognisable place that is not in the region. AVIF at a few widths, lazy below the first screen, inside the page-weight budget.
- **Home page, top to bottom:**
  1. the hero: a photo of a finished facade, one promise, and the two paths as two large buttons: *Prenova fasade* and *Nova fasada na ključ*;
  2. the process at a glance: the steps as a short numbered line;
  3. the facade systems in short, leading to *Vrste fasad*;
  4. the subsidy note: one sentence, a link to *Subvencije*;
  5. the quote request: what happens after it, and the button *Povpraševanje*;
  6. the footer with the e-mail and phone links and the legal pages.
- **Other pages:**
  - *Prenova fasade*: the six steps (visit and measurements, the offer, preparation of the scaffolding and the surface, insulation and reinforcement, the finishing coat and colour, the handover and the subsidy documents), what the owner does at each step, then the call to action;
  - *Nova fasada na ključ*: from the plan to the handover, and exactly what "na ključ" includes and what it doesn't;
  - *Vrste fasad*: contact (ETICS), ventilated and historic facades, and a comparison table (use, look, maintenance); no prices;
  - *Subvencije*: a short note on the Eko sklad subsidy for facade insulation, the link to the official call, and a "last checked" date;
  - *Pogosta vprašanja*: duration, permits, scaffolding, weather, payment, as an accordion that works without JavaScript;
  - *Povpraševanje*: the quote form, with the e-mail and phone links as the alternative.
- **The site's own components:** the two-path chooser, the numbered process line (vertical on a phone), the systems comparison table (scrolls inside its own frame on a phone), the FAQ accordion, the multi-step quote form, and a sticky bottom bar on phones with two actions (*Pokliči*, *Povpraševanje*).
- **Navigation:** Prenova fasade, Nova fasada na ključ, Vrste fasad, Subvencije, Pogosta vprašanja, and *Povpraševanje* as a button. On a phone the links fold into a menu that opens without JavaScript.
- **From the reference sites:**
  - izdelavafasade.si: the process placed right before the call to action, and the subsidy as its own section;
  - fasada.si: the dated guide format for *Subvencije*, and the form's questions (building age, facade area, time frame, help with the subsidy);
  - aroundhome.de/fassade: entry by job type that pre-fills the form, and "updated on" on the guide pages;
  - merkur.si (its turnkey facade service): saying exactly what "na ključ" includes and how long it takes.
- **Design rules,** checked by `pnpm test` where a machine can:
  - a photo shows at most once on a page;
  - every photo has alternative text; a decorative photo has empty alternative text;
  - stock photos only, chosen with licences that need no credit; no photo stands for a real job;
  - text never lies on the photo itself: it sits on a solid dark panel that passes 4.5:1 and the photo fades into it; the fade is a real element, not a `::before` or `::after` (axe reports text over a gradient as "needs review", which fails pa11y in CI);
  - every link and button names where it leads, never "Read more" alone;
  - no placeholder text (sample text, lorem ipsum, TBD, TODO) reaches any page, the legal pages included;
  - every fact (the subsidy, its conditions, its call number and dates) cites its source with the date it was checked; a fact that can't be checked against the official call is left out;
  - no prices, no warranty or response-time claims, no references, no partner logos;
  - every menu link leads to a finished page in the site's design;
  - no `style` attributes in the HTML; long words in headings break at 320px.

## Domains and environments

- **Main address:** fasadanakljuc.si. www.fasadanakljuc.si and http redirect to https://fasadanakljuc.si permanently at the Cloudflare edge.
- **Environments** on Cloudflare Pages: `dev` at dev.fasadanakljuc.si (every merge to `main`) and `staging` at staging.fasadanakljuc.si (release tags), both behind Cloudflare Access; `production` at production.fasadanakljuc.si behind sign-in until the cutover, then at fasadanakljuc.si, public. A release is promoted from `staging` after the release approver's sign-off. The steps are in the template's `docs/deployment.md`.
- **Edge:** fasadanakljuc.si becomes a new zone in Trislab's Cloudflare account (tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/fasadanakljuc.si/`); the domain resolves nowhere today. The zone holds the form sender's SPF and DKIM records. It is signed (DNSSEC) and blocks AI crawlers at the edge. Each environment has its Turnstile widget; the site key goes into the site's settings, the secret only to the form Worker (the template's `docs/forms.md`).
- **Search Console** (`search-console`): the domain property `sc-domain:fasadanakljuc.si`, owned by Trislab's role account. Trislab creates it and adds its verification record with the key `google` in tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/fasadanakljuc.si/fasadanakljuc.si.yaml`, linked in `catalog/domains.yaml`; keep the record through the cutover. The sitemap is `https://fasadanakljuc.si/sitemap-index.xml`: Trislab submits it after the cutover and checks the site in Search Console 4–6 weeks later.

## Integrations and outside services

- **The shared form Worker** (Trislab): receives the quote form, checks Turnstile and the fields, and e-mails the form to the company's mailbox. It stores nothing. Its address, fields and messages are set per site in tl-cloud-infrastructure; the form component is the template's (`docs/forms.md`).
- **Cloudflare Turnstile:** on the form page only; it sets no tracking cookie. The form page's own Content-Security-Policy rule allows it (the template's `docs/forms.md` section 3); every other page keeps the default policy.
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics:** cookieless visitor statistics on production only (`web_analytics: true`, the template's `docs/analytics.md`).
- **Google Maps:** a plain link only.
- The privacy policy names Cloudflare (the challenge, the statistics), the mail provider and the mailbox the form goes to.

## Quality baseline

The template's whole baseline, no exceptions: `responsive-layout` (mobile first, no sideways scrolling at 320px), `accessibility` (WCAG 2.1 AA and a statement), `performance-budget`, `page-weight-budget`, `image-optimisation`, `caching`, `compression`, `security-headers`, `error-pages`. The form is tested with a keyboard and a screen reader, Turnstile included. The template's claims ("no JavaScript", no third-party requests) are reviewed for the form page. Uptime alerts come when the platform has them. Browsers: current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every page in the table exists, with a canonical address and a sitemap entry.
- `ci` is green: the build, the page-weight budget, the browser check (no sideways scrolling at 320px, axe-core), the claims check, the design check (no photo twice on a page, no placeholder text) and the `security.txt` check.
- The quote form works without JavaScript; each step's errors are shown next to the field and read by a screen reader; a test submission on production.fasadanakljuc.si reaches the mailbox, and a submission without a valid Turnstile token is refused.
- The site sets no cookies; only the form page loads Turnstile, and only production loads Cloudflare Web Analytics.
- www.fasadanakljuc.si and http redirect permanently (301); an unknown address answers 404.
- Google's Rich Results Test reads the `LocalBusiness` and `FAQPage` data.
- SEO (owner: the development team, unless named): the Lighthouse SEO score passes in `ci`; every page has a title, a description and one share image with alt text; dev, staging and production.fasadanakljuc.si stay behind sign-in and out of search. At promotion (owner: the release approver): the go-live check on production.fasadanakljuc.si. After the cutover (owner: Trislab): the sitemap is submitted in Search Console.
- The design check: the release approver compares staging with this brief's design direction (palette, type, the home page's sections in order, the site's own components); nothing looks like the template's sample, the company's main site or its roof site.
- Before production: the subsidy facts are checked against the official Eko sklad call and dated, the legal pages are in place, and the release approver accepts staging.

## Open questions

- The subsidy facts (call, conditions, amounts) must be checked in the official call text before they are written; the call's pages could not be opened when the analysis was made.
- The search terms (prenova fasade, obnova fasade, izdelava fasade, fasada na ključ, Eko sklad subvencija fasada, regional facade terms) have no measured search volume yet; Trislab checks them with a keyword tool before the cutover.
- The form Worker and its template component are built first (shared code); the site's cutover waits for them.
